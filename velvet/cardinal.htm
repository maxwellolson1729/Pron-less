So, what is a Faro shuffle?

A perfect Faro shuffle starts by cutting a 52-card deck exactly in half: 26 cards and 26 cards. You then interleave the halves perfectly, alternating one card from each packet.

This puzzle uses an out-shuffle, which means the original top card stays on top. Do that eight times and the entire deck returns to exactly where it started. Cards are apparently very committed to the bit.
